# AI-Based Farmer Query Support and Advisory System
## Digital Krishi Officer - Always Available, Always Learning, Always Farmer-First

---

## 📋 Project Overview

The AI-Based Farmer Query Support and Advisory System is an innovative solution designed to bridge the critical gap between farmers and agricultural expertise. This system serves as a "Digital Krishi Officer" that provides instant, accurate, and context-aware agricultural advice to farmers in their native language, primarily Malayalam.

**Problem Statement ID:** 25076

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

### The Challenge
Farmers across India, particularly in Kerala, face numerous critical challenges daily:
- **Pest and Disease Management**: Identifying and treating crop diseases and pest infestations
- **Weather-Related Decisions**: Making informed decisions based on weather patterns
- **Input Management**: Selecting appropriate fertilizers, pesticides, and seeds
- **Subsidy Navigation**: Understanding and accessing government subsidies and schemes
- **Market Intelligence**: Getting timely information about market trends and pricing

### Current Limitations
- **Limited Access**: Agri officers and helplines are overburdened and cannot provide personalized support
- **Scalability Issues**: Existing services don't scale to support individual needs across diverse regions
- **Language Barriers**: Most agricultural information is available in English or Hindi, not local languages
- **Literacy Challenges**: Traditional text-based systems don't accommodate varying literacy levels
- **Timing**: Critical agricultural decisions often need immediate expert input that isn't available

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

Build an AI-powered advisory system that democratizes access to agricultural expertise by allowing farmers to ask queries in their own language and receive accurate, context-aware answers instantly.

### Vision
To create a comprehensive digital agricultural advisor that understands local contexts, speaks the farmer's language, and provides expert-level guidance 24/7.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. Natural Language Query Handling
- **Malayalam Support**: Primary focus on Malayalam language processing
- **Voice & Text Input**: Farmers can ask questions through voice commands or text
- **Example Queries**: 
  - "വാഴയിലെ ഇല പുള്ളി രോഗത്തിന് ഏത് കീടനാശിനി ഉപയോഗിക്കാം?" (Which pesticide for leaf spot in banana?)
  - "ഈ കാലാവസ്ഥയിൽ നെല്ല് നടാൻ പറ്റുമോ?" (Can I plant rice in this weather?)

### 2. Multimodal Input Support
- **Image Recognition**: Upload photos of diseased crops, pests, or soil conditions
- **Voice Notes**: Record and send voice messages for complex queries
- **Video Support**: Short video clips for better context understanding
- **GPS Integration**: Automatic location detection for region-specific advice

### 3. AI-Powered Knowledge Engine
- **LLM Integration**: Leveraging large language models fine-tuned on agricultural datasets
- **Local Knowledge Base**: Integration with:
  - Local crop calendars
  - Regional pest advisories
  - Weather pattern data
  - Government scheme guidelines
  - Market price information
- **Continuous Learning**: System improves with each interaction

### 4. Context Awareness
- **Location-Based Advice**: Recommendations based on farmer's geographical location
- **Crop-Specific Guidance**: Tailored advice for specific crops being grown
- **Seasonal Intelligence**: Time-sensitive recommendations based on current season
- **Historical Context**: Learning from farmer's previous queries and farming history
- **Soil Type Consideration**: Advice based on local soil conditions

### 5. Escalation System
- **Smart Routing**: Complex queries automatically escalated to local agri officers
- **Context Preservation**: Full query context and AI suggestions provided to human experts
- **Priority Handling**: Urgent issues (disease outbreaks, weather emergencies) get immediate attention
- **Expert Network**: Connection to specialized experts for specific crops or issues

### 6. Learning Loop
- **Feedback Integration**: Continuous improvement based on farmer feedback
- **Expert Validation**: Local agricultural experts validate and improve AI responses
- **Real-World Outcomes**: System learns from actual farming results and success stories
- **Community Knowledge**: Farmers can share successful practices with the community
